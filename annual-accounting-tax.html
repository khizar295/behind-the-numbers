How we can help

Reliable annual accounting, explained clearly

Annual accounting is more than preparing a set of figures once a year. It is an opportunity to review how your business has performed, make sure records are brought together and understand the financial and tax position of the business.

We work with business owners to organise relevant financial information, prepare agreed annual reporting and explain the key points in plain language. The exact work depends on your business structure, activities and circumstances.

Whether you operate as a sole trader, partnership, company or trust, having a clear view of your annual financial position can help you plan ahead and approach important decisions with better information.

  • Annual financial statements
  • Income tax return preparation
  • GST compliance support
  • Fringe benefit tax support, where relevant
  • Tax planning discussions
  • Support for companies, trusts and sole traders

More than year-end paperwork

Understand the story behind your business numbers

Your annual accounts can tell you much more than whether your business made a profit or loss. They can help you understand how the business is performing, where money is being spent and how your financial position has changed over the year.

Taking time to review these results can help you identify questions to explore, prepare for upcoming obligations and consider what your business may need in the year ahead.

Be prepared

What should you have ready?

Having complete and organised records can make the annual process smoother. The information required will vary depending on your business, accounting system and the services agreed.

If you are unsure whether something is relevant, ask before spending time preparing it. We can help identify what is needed for your circumstances.

You do not need to have everything perfectly organised before getting in touch. We can discuss the records you already have and identify any information that may still be required.

Common information may include

  • Bank statements and reconciliations
  • Sales, income and expense records
  • Invoices, receipts and supporting documents
  • Details of business assets, loans and finance
  • Payroll, GST and other relevant records
  • Information about significant business changes

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

A few common questions about annual accounting and tax support.

What is included in annual accounting?

The scope can include annual financial statements, income tax return preparation and discussion of relevant compliance matters. The exact deliverables depend on your entity type, circumstances and agreed engagement.

When should I start preparing for year-end?

It is helpful to keep records up to date throughout the year rather than waiting until year-end. Contact your accountant early if you expect unusual transactions, changes to your business or difficulty meeting a deadline.

Can you work with my existing accounting software?

We can discuss the accounting system you currently use and the records available. The most suitable process will depend on the software, the quality of the records and the services required.

Do you provide advice as well as preparing returns?

Yes, practical discussions can form part of the agreed service. If you have a specific tax or business question, raise it with us so we can confirm the advice required and whether any additional work is needed.

What if my business records are not fully organised?

You can still get in touch. We can discuss the records you have, identify what information may be missing and agree on the next steps needed for the work.

Do you work with small businesses?

We provide accounting and tax support to Auckland's SME community. Get in touch to discuss your business structure, requirements and the support you are looking for.
