Who we are

A practice focused on the people behind the numbers

Good accounting is about more than completing forms or producing reports. It is about helping business owners understand what their financial information means, what needs attention and what decisions may be worth considering.

Clear information. Practical conversations.

Behind the Numbers works with Auckland’s SME community through annual accounting and tax services, business advisory and management reporting, and day-to-day GST, payroll and bookkeeping support.

Our approach is centred on making financial information easier to understand. We aim to explain the important points in plain language and connect the figures to the realities of running a business.

Our purpose

Helping business owners move forward with confidence

Business owners make decisions every day about costs, people, customers, investment and growth. Reliable financial information can make those decisions clearer.

Clarity

Understand your financial position, obligations and the movements that matter.

Support

Have an accounting partner to discuss questions and practical next steps.

Perspective

Use financial information to plan ahead rather than only looking backwards.

Our approach

Accounting that connects financial information with real business decisions

Every business has different financial priorities. Some owners need help maintaining accurate records, while others want a clearer understanding of profitability, cash flow and future financial commitments.

At Behind the Numbers, our approach is centred on understanding the business behind the financial information. We consider your circumstances, reporting requirements and objectives when providing accounting and advisory support.

Our aim is to make accounting information more useful, understandable and relevant to the decisions you make throughout the year.

Our professional commitment

Careful work and clear communication

Accounting and tax matters can have important consequences. We take a considered approach to the information provided, the obligations relevant to each engagement and the need to explain matters clearly.
