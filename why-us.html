Why Behind the Numbers

A practical approach to business accounting

Behind the Numbers is a licensed Chartered Accountancy public practice supporting Auckland's SME community. Our service areas bring together annual accounting and tax, business advisory and management reporting, and day-to-day GST, payroll and bookkeeping support.

Clarity over complexity

We aim to explain financial information in plain language and make important points easier to follow.

Communication

A conversation helps us understand your business and explain the information or next steps relevant to the work.

A forward-looking view

Reporting and planning can help you consider what may be ahead, not only what has already happened.

Understand your numbers

Financial information should help you make decisions

Financial statements and reports are most useful when you understand what they say about your business. We focus on connecting figures with the questions owners need to answer.

From figures to useful insight

A change in revenue, gross margin, overheads or cash balance can have different causes. Reviewing movements and asking the right questions can help identify where further attention may be useful.

Business visibility

See the bigger picture behind your business numbers

Understanding your financial position involves more than looking at a bank balance. Revenue, expenses, profitability and cash flow each provide a different perspective on how your business is performing.

With appropriate accounting information and reporting, business owners can review financial movements, identify areas that need attention and consider decisions with greater confidence.

Profitability

Review income and expenses to understand where business performance is changing.

Cash Flow

Understand the movement of money coming into and going out of your business.

Financial Position

Review assets, liabilities and other information that contribute to your overall financial position.

Planning

Use financial information to consider budgets, priorities and future business requirements.

Working together

Good accounting starts with a collaborative relationship

Accounting support works best when information is shared clearly and responsibilities are understood. Both the business owner and accounting practice have an important part to play.

Your business

  • Provide complete and relevant financial records.
  • Share important changes in your business circumstances.
  • Respond to information requests and queries.
  • Raise questions when financial information is unclear.

Our practice

  • Explain relevant accounting information and requirements.
  • Carry out work within the agreed engagement scope.
  • Identify relevant financial matters for discussion.
  • Communicate relevant outcomes and follow-up requirements.

Local SME focus

Advice that recognises the realities of running a business

Small and medium-sized businesses operate with different resources and pressures. Owners may be managing customers, employees, suppliers and cash flow at the same time. Accounting support should fit the context of the business rather than add unnecessary complexity.

Auckland-based practice

Behind the Numbers is located in Royal Oak, Auckland. Our focus is on supporting Auckland's SME community with accounting, tax and business advisory services.
