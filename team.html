Working with you

A professional relationship built on understanding

Behind the Numbers is a licensed Chartered Accountancy public practice serving Auckland's SME community. Our work is centred on understanding each client's business and providing accounting, tax and advisory support that is relevant to the agreed engagement.

A team approach to your finances

The website information supplied for this practice does not publish individual staff biographies or names. Rather than introduce unverified profiles, this page explains the professional approach and service responsibilities you can expect when working with Behind the Numbers.

Professional standards

Professional care in every engagement

Accounting is about more than preparing reports. It requires professional judgement, attention to detail and a clear understanding of the responsibilities involved in each engagement.

Professional judgement

Consider the relevant financial information and circumstances when working through accounting and tax matters.

Professional responsibility

Approach client work with an understanding of applicable accounting requirements and professional obligations.

Attention to detail

Review financial records and relevant information carefully as part of the agreed accounting work.

Professional development

Keeping knowledge relevant in a changing business environment

Accounting, taxation and business requirements can change over time. Staying informed about relevant developments is an important part of providing responsible professional services.

Behind the Numbers recognises the importance of applying relevant professional knowledge when working with clients on accounting, taxation and business advisory matters.

Annual accounting

Support for year-end responsibilities

Annual accounting work involves bringing relevant financial information together, preparing statements and addressing income tax requirements. The process depends on the records available and the nature of the business.

What this service area covers

Annual Accounting and Tax support includes financial statements, income tax returns and practical tax guidance. The aim is to help clients understand their annual position and the obligations relevant to their circumstances.

Day-to-day support

Keeping important financial processes moving

Bookkeeping, GST and payroll processes help maintain the records that support reporting and annual accounting. Consistency is important, especially when business owners have limited time for administration.

A connected service

The practice's GST, Payroll and Bookkeeping service area includes bookkeeping, GST preparation and support, payroll assistance, Xero setup and support, bank reconciliations and accounts payable and receivable processes.

Our workflow

Connecting everyday records with bigger business decisions

Good financial management relies on accurate records, meaningful reporting and an understanding of what the numbers reveal. These activities can work together to give business owners a clearer view of their financial position.

01

Maintain financial records

Bookkeeping, bank reconciliations and transaction records help maintain an organised financial foundation.

02

Prepare and review reports

Financial statements, GST information and management reports help explain business activity and performance.

03

Support informed decisions

Financial insights can help business owners consider budgets, cash flow, costs and future priorities.
