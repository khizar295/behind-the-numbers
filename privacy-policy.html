Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Behind the Numbers Limited may collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website or get in touch with us.

Last updated: November 2026

Our commitment to your privacy

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable New Zealand privacy requirements, including the Privacy Act 2020.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the website operated by Behind the Numbers Limited ("we", "us" or "our") and explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and related communications.

By using our website or choosing to provide information to us, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this policy. This policy should be read alongside any separate engagement terms or privacy notices that apply when you become a client.

This policy relates to website and general enquiry information. Information handled while providing accounting, taxation or advisory services may be subject to additional professional, contractual and legal obligations.

2. Information We Collect

The type of information we collect depends on how you interact with our website and communicate with us. It may include:

Contact information

Your name, email address, phone number and other contact details you choose to provide.

Enquiry information

The details and messages you submit when requesting a consultation or contacting our team.

Technical information

Information such as browser type, device details, IP address and website activity, where collected by our systems.

Business information

Business details or other information you voluntarily provide when discussing your accounting or advisory needs.

Please avoid sending sensitive financial records, identity documents or confidential information through general website forms unless we have specifically requested them and provided an appropriate method for secure submission.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information in several ways, including:

  • When you complete a contact or consultation enquiry form.
  • When you email, telephone or otherwise communicate with us.
  • When you voluntarily provide information during an initial business discussion.
  • Through technical systems that support website operation and security.
  • Through service providers that support our website or communications, where applicable.

Where practical, we will explain why information is being requested and whether providing it is optional or necessary for us to respond to your enquiry.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for purposes connected with our business and your interaction with us, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Communicating with you about our services.
  • Understanding your accounting, taxation or business advisory requirements.
  • Operating, maintaining and improving our website.
  • Protecting our website, systems and business from misuse or security threats.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

We will not knowingly use personal information for a purpose that is incompatible with the reason it was collected, unless permitted or required by law.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary for our business operations or where legally permitted or required.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Technology, hosting and website support providers.
  • Professional advisers and service providers assisting us with our operations.
  • Government agencies, regulators or other authorities where required by law.
  • Other parties where you have authorised the disclosure or where disclosure is otherwise legally permitted.

Where a service provider handles information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate arrangements are in place for handling that information.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, misuse, modification or disclosure.

Depending on the information and systems involved, safeguards may include access controls, secure service providers, password protection and appropriate internal procedures.

No method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you believe information you have provided to us has been compromised, please contact us promptly.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for as long as required by applicable legal, regulatory, professional or business obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete it or de-identify it, where appropriate and permitted by law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Under New Zealand privacy law, you may have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct information that you believe is inaccurate.

If you wish to make a request, please:

  • Contact us using the details at the end of this policy.
  • Tell us what information you are requesting or would like corrected.
  • Provide enough information for us to identify the relevant records and respond to your request.

We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements. In some circumstances, the law may allow or require us to withhold certain information.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy concern, you may also contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Office of the Privacy Commissioner

9. Cookies and Website Data

Our website or the services supporting it may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website performance or improve the visitor experience.

The information collected through these technologies depends on the tools and services enabled on the website. You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function as intended if certain cookies are disabled.

If analytics, advertising or other tracking services are used, this policy should be updated to identify those services and explain their relevant data practices.

10. Links to Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including government agencies, professional organisations and social media platforms.

These websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy, security or content of external websites. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
